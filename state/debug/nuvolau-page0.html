Rifugio Nuvolau features

Rifugio Nuvolau is a simple traditional hut reachable only on foot
  • Rustic dorms
  • Shared toilets, no showers
  • Single or bunk beds only, in shared dorms
  • Pillows and blankets are provided
  • Water and electricity are limited resources and not always available: here, every drop of water is precious.

Overnight stay options

  • Bed only
  • Bed&breakfast
  • Half board: dinner, bed and breakfast
  • Dinner is served at 7 PM, breakfast is available between 7 AM and 8 AM
  • Vegetarian options available Vegan options not available
  • When booking you can select the bed(s) and the preferred stay option
  • The Rifugio reserves the right to reassign rooms for organizational reasons
  • Check-in from 4 PM, check-out before 9 AM
  • Any special request should be specified in the ‘Special requests’ section when booking.

Rules and coditions

  • No pets allowed
  • Group bookings: maximum 8 people at standard price
  • Due to logistic reasons, we don’t accept bookings made by agencies.
  • Rifugio Nuvolau belongs to the CAI (Italian Alpine Club) Cortina section therefore the CAI hut regulations always apply.
  • Discounts:
    • CAI (Italian Alpine Club) members: -20% (upon presentation at check-out of membership card valid for the current year, as per CAI regulations)
    • Under 18: -30% (applied at the time of final payment at the Rifugio)
    • Members of other alpine clubs: members of foreign alpine clubs, are also entitled to the discount, on condition that their membership card, valid for the current year, shows the reciprocal agreement symbol.

Bookings

  • Bookings can be made exclusively through the booking system on the website
  • To confirm the booking, the payment of a deposit of 40% of the total amount is required
  • The deposit payment is processed directly on the website during the booking process; all major credit cards are accepted
  • The balance of the booking will be paid at the Rifugio at check-out

Our dorms

Here, at 2575m of altitude, simplicity is an added value: rustic dorms with single or bunk beds, shared toilets and no showers, because water is precious here and the mountain decides its availability. You can choose between overnight stay, half board or bed&breakfast, share the spaces and rediscover the meaning of the word “essential“.
The Rifugio has 24 beds and can accept groups of max. 8 people.

3-BED DORM

Bed in a shared 3-bed dorm. Pillows and blankets are provided. Shared toilets.

TWO 4-BED DORMS

Bed in a shared 4-bed dorm. Pillows and blankets are provided. Shared toilets.

5-BED DORM

Bed in a shared 5-bed dorm. Pillows and blankets are provided. Shared toilets.

8-BED DORM

Bed in a shared 8-bed dorm. Pillows and blankets are provided. Shared toilets.
